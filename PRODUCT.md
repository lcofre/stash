# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing personal lists (to watch, to read, to buy, to do, to research) on their phone or desktop. They want to install the app once and access their data instantly, offline, with no account needed. They use it throughout the day, often in quick bursts.

## Product Purpose

Stash is a lightweight, offline-first categorized todo app that respects privacy and speed. No server, no signup, no tracking. Users choose their own categories and organize what matters to them. The app scales from simple todos to rich lists (movies with ratings, books with covers, links with notes).

Success: users feel the app is responsive, clear, and trustworthy — like it's a tool built for them, not trying to sell them something.

## Brand Personality

**Refined, direct, trustworthy.**

The app speaks in a lowercase, understated voice. It shows things as they are, without hype. Dark, sophisticated, with warm amber accents. Clean lines. Serif typography for personality; sans-serif for clarity. No friction, no filler.

## Anti-references

- Overly decorative or ornamental designs (excessive grain, heavy shadows, unnecessary flourishes)
- SaaS-style onboarding or marketing language inside the app
- Trendy glassmorphism or gradient effects
- Busy layouts with too much visual noise
- Playful or cute tone (aim for elegant instead)

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity first** — Information should be instantly readable. Typography, spacing, and hierarchy should guide the eye without confusion.
2. **Modern minimalism** — Sophistication through subtraction, not addition. Every element earns its space. Clean and direct.
3. **Mobile is primary** — The app feels sharp and accessible on a 4" phone screen. Generous touch targets, readable text, no tiny details.
4. **Trustworthy restraint** — Dark theme with amber accents. Use color deliberately. Let whitespace breathe.
5. **Invisible craftsmanship** — Users notice that things work smoothly and feel polished, not that the design is "designed."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast ratios throughout
- Sufficient touch target sizes (minimum 44x44 on mobile)
- Readable text sizes on all screen sizes, especially mobile
- Clear visual hierarchy so important actions don't get lost
